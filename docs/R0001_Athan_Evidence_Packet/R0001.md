## 1. Executive decision and intended outcome

Repair the meaning of the solar state across the observatory, not merely its appearance. One UTC instant, observer definition and selected prayer policy must produce mutually consistent solar geometry, physical event states, assigned prayer times, map fronts, pulses, inspector values and continuity statistics. The illumination model must distinguish direct solar light, atmospheric scattering and artificial emission, with a correct linear-light/display pipeline.

**The initial suspicion that axial tilt or ecliptic longitude is absent is rejected.** The existing `solar.ts` includes apparent solar longitude, corrected obliquity, declination and equation of time. The verified failures are principally in event semantics, front direction, temporal/configuration integration and rendering. Preserve that useful astronomy rather than replacing it on a false premise. [S01]

The decisive example is Tromsø in winter: no sunrise or sunset, yet midday twilight; the system must not fabricate a sunrise, sunset or ordinary solar-shadow Asr to keep every field a `Date`. Its summer counterpart must represent midnight sun without a fictitious sunset. Valid twilight-angle crossings can still exist during polar night: absence of sunrise does not erase every other astronomical sign or decide the religious policy.

This is one work-order with separable implementation cells and a mandatory integration acceptance. An urgent event/front repair may merge before the renderer, with explicit partial status; it cannot close R0001. Adopting SPA alone, recolouring the terminator, returning `null` without repairing consumers, or merely weakening the README does not satisfy this contract.

### Why this happens

The weakest supported common cause is a missing cross-layer semantic contract. The application treats a mandatory clock value, a physical crossing, a fiqh assignment, a coloured contour and a lighting blend as interchangeable representations of the same thing. The type surface cannot express some real outcomes; tests check the resulting simplified shape or reuse the same sign convention; state changes then reach only some consumers.

This is not a diagnosis of the author's competence or intentions. It is a traceable architecture/evaluator failure family. A provider swap cannot repair consumers that erase absence, a successful test cannot validate an inverted test oracle, and a plausible image cannot establish physical transport.

## 7. Physical and temporal contracts

### 7.1 Shared solar state and frames

One astronomy owner supplies absolute instant, provider/version/range, time-scale assumptions, Earth-fixed Sun direction and the intermediates/corrections needed for the admitted accuracy. An SPA route must bind TT/UT1, ΔT/DUT1 and geocentric/topocentric assumptions; `Date` alone is not a precision time-scale model. Match reference quantities: unrefracted observer altitude is distinct from refracted altitude, and solar aberration/nutation corrections are a separate distinction. Do not mix incompatible RA epochs and sidereal-time conventions. [P03]

Preserve display axes: `+Y` north, prime meridian `+Z`, east-positive longitude towards `+X` on the equator. Map standard ECEF axes explicitly if adopted. Share geocentric ephemeris per required instant and apply observer corrections separately; no repeated full SPA where a shared state suffices. Document the spherical/geodetic approximation rather than equating radial normals with ellipsoidal observer verticals. Existing obliquity is a positive control. [S01], [S24]

### 7.2 Missing events remain results

Over a named definition and half-open absolute interval `[start,end)`, preserve these semantic outcomes, however the final TypeScript types are arranged:

```text
crossings: individually directed roots, each with bracket/error
alwaysAbove / alwaysBelow: established for this model and interval only
grazing/contact: threshold touched without an ordinary directed crossing
indeterminate: insufficient bounds/data to decide topology
invalid/unsupported: input, provider or numerical failure
```

One civil day may have only a rise or a set. Handle exact poles, seasonal transitions and endpoints explicitly. Analytic hour angles may seed/bound the search; evaluate the time-varying Sun at final candidate times and refine bracketed roots using a safeguarded solver. Include extrema/contact detection: coarse sign changes miss tangency or short daylight. Never clamp impossible `cos(H)` into an event. A justified floating-point roundoff clamp is not an excuse to erase physical absence or uncertainty.

### 7.3 Distinct boundaries

**Geometric terminator:** centre altitude `0°`, a great circle for a spherical Earth/distant point Sun. **Conventional apparent sunrise/sunset:** approximately `-0.8333°` unrefracted centre altitude at an unobstructed sea-level horizon, comprising about 16 arcminutes solar radius plus 34 arcminutes mean refraction. Elevation, terrain and atmosphere can change it. **Twilight classifications:** centre depressions 6°, 12°, 18°; definitions, not brightness switches. [P01], [P02]

Finite-disk visibility, refraction, scattered light and pixel antialiasing soften different boundaries for different reasons. They need not coincide. `smoothstep` is allowed for a justified approximation, not as an unnamed replacement for all four.

### 7.4 Dates and event windows

Settlement schedules take explicit **civil date plus IANA zone** and return UTC instants. Analysis windows are absolute; civil days may span 23/25 hours. For coordinates without a zone, label a UTC/selected-zone view; do not infer political zones from longitude or use the viewer's zone silently. Reject invalid dates, non-finite/out-of-range coordinates, unknown conventions and invalid zones.

Active/next-event queries enumerate every civil date capable of contributing to the requested window, including adjacent dates. Recompute next-day ephemerides, never add 24h to today's Fajr or fold dates modulo 86400. Use stable event identities and `[event,event+duration)` membership. Exhausted bounded searches remain unresolved/search-limit, not invented next prayers.

## 8. Prayer policy, fronts and configuration

### 8.1 Assignment provenance and policy boundaries

Each prayer result retains key, civil date, assigned instant or unresolved state, source sign(s), convention/version, madhab, policy and adjustment/reason. Distinguish **astronomical-sign**, **configured fixed-interval**, **explicit high-latitude adjustment** and **policy-required/unresolved** derivations. These are engineering provenance labels, not religious-validity grades.

An adjusted prayer can coexist with no physical sunrise; it cannot make sunrise true. Missing sunrise must not suppress other valid twilight crossings or transit. Preserve configured values unless sources and maintainer approval warrant change. Disposition all ten conventions and both madhabs; a name such as `UmmAlQura` does not prove complete official-calendar conformity. No silent Ramadan, nearest-latitude/day or universal polar rule.

For `AngleBased`, adopt an explicit formula, sources, prerequisites and capping behaviour, or mark it unsupported. Angle/60 of the applicable night is a candidate requiring approval, not a fiat in this issue. Use the correct neighbouring sunset/sunrise; absence of that pair remains a prerequisite failure. Unknown policy cannot fall through to half-night. [S02], [S03]

### 8.2 Asr and Dhuhr

Ordinary Asr requires an illuminated upright gnomon and a valid local-noon shadow. Check those prerequisites before the formula; a negative polar-night `tan(abs(phi-delta))` is not a physical shadow. Solve its actual afternoon branch using the accepted noon reference/factor; retain no-sign when absent. Remove unlabelled `dhuhr+2h`.

A transit meridian is not scheduled Dhuhr when a safety offset applies. Show the assigned locus or label the line as transit and disclose the offset. Explicit zero offsets must survive truthiness defaults.

### 8.3 Event/front correspondence

Use the scheduler's definitions/configuration. Label geometric, apparent, twilight, ordinary-prayer and adjusted representations distinctly. Test altitude residual **and temporal direction**, valid Asr noon reference, and actual antecedent/interval for Isha. An adjusted policy may use a supported scheduled-time locus or labelled settlement points, never a fictitious physical twilight front.

Retain connected components/curve order rather than bridging filtered gaps. Test dateline wrapping, pole degeneracy and coverage beyond the current ±80°/±88° caps; explicit non-line representations are permissible. A complete diagnostic altitude ring remains valid even when a selected city has no crossing. [S04], [S05]

### 8.4 Shared receiving state

Use one small immutable configuration/revision, not a new framework. Give the inspector an update path instead of captured defaults. URL restoration, controls, day rollover, reverse scrubbing and dataset changes must reach all affected consumers.

## 9. Physical illumination, colour and atmosphere

### 9.1 Contributions and visibility

Use a decomposition equivalent to:

```text
surface radiance = reflected direct solar + reflected sky/indirect + emission
camera radiance = view transmittance * surface radiance + atmospheric in-scattering
```

A Lambertian direct baseline is `albedo/pi * solarIrradiance * sunTransmittance * visibility * max(dot(shadingNormal,sunDirection),0)`. Declare physical units or consistent relative radiance. An ocean BRDF changes local reflection, not planetary visibility. [P05], [P06]

Use actual position/reference geometry for occlusion and perturbed normals only for reflection. Renormalise blended normals; handle zero-vector/pole tangents before normalisation. Specular/cloud sunlight cannot bypass visibility through arbitrary offsets. Remove the direct diffuse floor; artificial lights remain an independent emissive contribution. Uncalibrated stock images do not become measured albedo/night radiance by loading them into PBR.

Straight point-Sun rays intersecting Earth are blocked. A finite disk uses its visible fraction with stated approximation error. An apparent/refractive profile has a separately documented visibility model; do not double-count refraction. **Negative centre altitude alone does not imply zero direct light** when the upper limb/refracted disk is visible. Atmosphere/elevated cloud samples have their own visibility: ground shadow cannot mask all high-altitude illumination.

### 9.2 Linear-light/display contract

Decode colour textures once; treat normal/specular/density maps as data under declared encodings. Preserve the existing day/night sRGB annotations. Classify cloud texture use instead of assuming a density mask needs sRGB conversion.

Sum physical light/composite in linear space, then tone-map and encode exactly once. Direct-to-canvas custom shaders need the appropriate Three.js conversion/tone-mapping path; renderer settings alone do not insert those operations. With HDR multipass, use final output conversion rather than adding independently tone-mapped layers. Intentionally un-tonemapped overlays still need coherent colour output. [P04]

Test linear black/white mixing (`0.5` linear ≈ `0.735` sRGB with tone mapping disabled), encoded-grey round trips, missing/double transfer, data-map invariance, exposure monotonicity and custom/built-in reference equivalence. These are required new diagnostics, **not measured baseline pixels**.

### 9.3 Minimum physical atmosphere

Physical mode needs spherical solar/view paths, altitude-dependent density, extinction/transmittance, Rayleigh and aerosol/Mie scattering, and stated phase-function/spectral-to-RGB approximations. Couple sky irradiance to shadowed surfaces; twilight is not just a painted limb.

Compare a Bruneton-style precomputed implementation with a bounded analytic/numerical alternative. Choose against reference/error and resource budgets, not names of shader variables. Explicitly disposition multiple scattering, ozone and spectral resolution as supported/approximated/deferred. A clear-sky RGB approximation makes no weather-calibrated or whole-spectrum claim. [P05], [P06]

Freeze clear-sky parameters and compare with an independent/higher-quality reference across Sun altitude, camera altitude and view direction. Cover winter civil twilight, sunset, continuous twilight, night limb and lit upper atmosphere over shadowed ground. Zero medium recovers vacuum; blocked direct light can coexist with scattered light. Preserve the distinction numerically, not just in screenshots.

Frame-driven cloud rotation must be labelled decorative or any claimed time-reproducible evolution must use simulation time. No weather-model expansion. Cartographic style may simplify appearance but cannot change astronomical/prayer results.

## 15. Counterarguments, regression and rollback

Existing astronomy may already be adequate; preserve it if qualified. A large scattering implementation may exceed its value; compare bounded alternatives and measured budgets. Neither point excuses false events, mirrored fronts or inconsistent consumers. Avoid framework replacement, duplicate permanent engines and ornamental registries.

Compatibility protects valid supported behaviour, not known-wrong dates. An adapter must retain missing-event semantics. Preserve a coherent preimage and evidence before each change; revert a cell with its dependent adapters/tests, not half a configuration. A safe partial release may retain corrected astronomy and disable/label unqualified rendering; it does not close R0001.

Retiring a dependency/model removes its dispatch, LUT/assets, lock entries and public claims together while retaining provenance. STOP on changed source assumptions, missing rights, contradictory policy, failed reference correspondence, unsupported input, uncontrolled cost or evaluator drift. Environment failure blocks an evidence leg; it proves neither target failure nor repair success.

- **P01:** US Naval Observatory, rise/set/transit/twilight definitions, upper-limb/refraction convention and horizon uncertainty.
- **P02:** NOAA, *General Solar Position Calculations*, east-positive longitude/solar-time and rise/set equations. Its separate sunrise-formula hour-angle sign is not a licence to invert the local hour-angle definition used in this issue.
- **P03:** Official SPA site, hosted at the NLR domain at inspection; Reda/Andreas report metadata, precision statement and software redistribution notice. Also inspect its linked `spa.h` for time-scale and geometric/apparent/topocentric inputs/outputs. No acceptance of its licence terms or download of restricted source is authorised here.
- **P04:** Three.js official colour-management manual: linear working space, texture roles, display conversion and custom shader responsibilities. Verify against locked Three.js 0.180.0 during implementation.
- **P05:** Eric Bruneton's documented precomputed-scattering implementation, approximation/testing strategy and WebGL2 precedent; candidate, not a mandated dependency.
- **P06:** Pharr/Jakob/Humphreys, *Physically Based Rendering*, volume-scattering concepts and transmittance; not a requirement to build a path tracer.
- **P07:** NOAA calculation details, approximation/atmospheric limits and unmaintained online-calculator notice.
